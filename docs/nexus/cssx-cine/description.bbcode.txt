Hey everyone. CINE is a CSSX extension for making showcase videos of your character. You pick a preset, CINE moves the camera (and walks your character if the preset needs it), and you record it with whatever recorder you already use: OBS, ShadowPlay, Steam, anything. CINE doesn't record video itself.

With CSS (the wardrobe) installed, CINE can also change your outfits, skins, palettes and pieces during the shot, timed to music, and put your own look back afterwards. Without CSS the camera still works fine.

[size=4][b]Music scenes[/b][/size]
CINE plays scenes: presets timed to a song that play the music from the roll and cut the camera on its beat while CSS changes the outfit, the skin, the palette and the pieces with the song. Your own look comes back when the scene ends. Scenes come as separate downloads, one per song, with the music included:
[list]
[*][b]CINE Scenes: Iris Out[/b]: the Iris Out dance across the bones, Eve, Unholy Genessa and Commander White, in eight versions.
[*][b]CINE Scenes: Something Ain't Right[/b]: Showreel 124, Showreel Finale and the Commander White release cut.
[*][b]CINE Scenes: They Know Not What They Do[/b]: a close cut for Profane Proxima.
[/list]
Each pack unzips into the CINE folder and lists the outfits it needs.

[size=4][b]Camera presets[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Fashion Walk 360[/b]: she walks a route you recorded while the camera circles her once, then it settles on the last shot you framed yourself.
[*][b]Pose Glide 3 / 5 / 7 / 10 s[/b]: one smooth arc for idle poses, measured from her head so it works standing, sitting and kneeling.
[*][b]Turntable[/b]: a slow circle at full-body distance. [b]Photo Orbit[/b]: holds the camera on 7 angles so you can grab stills.
[*][b]Fight: Duel[/b], [b]Fight: Hero Orbit[/b], [b]Fight: Over the Shoulder[/b]: film a fight you play yourself. A kill plays in slow motion, a parry or a hit pushes the camera in.
[*][b]Breakdown Turntable[/b]: two slow circles while the picture is taken apart layer by layer.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Breakdown[/b][/size]
Show any take in layers like a VFX breakdown reel: clay, wireframe, skeleton, virtual / IK / spring bones, sockets, physics bodies, the movement capsule and frame passes (base colour, normals, depth). The frame can split on the middle of her body, mesh and rig on one side and the finished look on the other, with labels and arrows. CSS outfits with physics get their spring bones marked.

[size=4][b]Clean scene[/b][/size]
Hide enemies, or enemies and NPCs, while you're in Cine World, without pausing the world. They're frozen and can't be hit, new ones don't spawn, and everyone comes back as they were when you leave. Bosses are left alone. "Keep my lock-on target" leaves the enemy you're locked on to, for a clean one on one.

[size=4][b]Other stuff[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Walk takes use the game's real walk, not the scripted jog.
[*]The game HUD is hidden in Cine World. Frame guides (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 2.39:1) help you frame before you roll.
[*]Before a take, CINE checks the camera path for walls and pulls the camera in where it would clip through one.
[*]A countdown before each take so you have time to hit record.
[*]Your look goes back even when a take gets cut off by a loading screen, travel or death.
[*]Presets are plain .cine.json files in the presets folder. Copy one, tweak it, hit Reload presets.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How to use it[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Open CSSX (F6, or the CSSX tab in the Player Menu) and go to CINE.
[*]For a walk shot: Route > Record route. Walk the path you want, then stand still for 3 seconds.
[*]Put her where you want the shot, frame her with the normal game camera, and use Route > Use the current view as final shot (or F7 in Cine World).
[*]Pick a preset, hit Enter Cine World, start your recorder, press F8.
[/list]

For scenes, set Look track to As set in CSS, and Breakdown track to From the preset for the ones that stage the rig.

Keys in Cine World: [b]F8[/b] rolls a take, [b]F9[/b] cancels a take or leaves Cine World, [b]F10[/b] cycles the frame guide, [b]F7[/b] saves the final shot. Keyboard only, because the D-pad uses items in this game.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]You need CSSX 1.3.1 or newer.
[*]For the look track and the scenes: CSS 1.0.5 or newer.
[*]Close the game. Extract the [b]eins0fx.cine[/b] folder into [b]ue4ss/Mods/CSSX/extensions/[/b], and the [b]CSSX_CINE_eins0fx_P[/b] folder into [b]MortalShell2/Content/Paks/~mods/[/b].
[/list]

[code]Sparta/MortalShell2/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods/CSSX/extensions/
`-- eins0fx.cine/
    |-- README.txt
    |-- cine.dll
    |-- extension.json
    |-- menu.json
    |-- signature.txt
    |-- fonts/
    `-- presets/

Sparta/MortalShell2/Content/Paks/~mods/
`-- CSSX_CINE_eins0fx_P/
    |-- CSSX_CINE_eins0fx_P.pak
    |-- CSSX_CINE_eins0fx_P.ucas
    `-- CSSX_CINE_eins0fx_P.utoc[/code]

Don't add an extra folder level around them. Updating from 1.0.0: replace the eins0fx.cine folder and copy your own presets back in. Scene packs go on top of CINE; when you update CINE later, extract them again.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Opening a menu, pressing F9, a loading screen, travel or death all cancel a take. The camera, the walk and your look go back.
[*]On walk takes she just follows the path you recorded. Turn on Clean scene or clear the enemies first, and record over open ground.
[/list]
