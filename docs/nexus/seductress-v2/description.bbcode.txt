I expanded my Seductress adaptation into nine selectable variants, with revised body proportions, four color palettes and per-part customization. Choose a variant in CSS, adjust its colors and save the combination as a template.

[b]Requires Custom Shell System 0.2.0 or newer and UE4SS. Includes nude variants.[/b]

This is a separate outfit package. It appears as [b]Seductress V2[/b] in the wardrobe and can stay installed alongside the original Seductress. Your current shell's abilities stay the same.

[size=4][b]What's included[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Nine mesh variants: Original, Open Skirt, Harness, Topless Gown, Topless Harness, Unveiled, Bare Jewels, Bare Winged and Bare Claws.
[*]Revised body proportions and garment fit.
[*]Original colors, plus Crimson vow, Ash and gold, Midnight silver and Verdigris.
[*]Separate colors for Garment, Ornaments, Skin, Mask and Eyes, plus eye glow intensity.
[*]Support for CSS favorites and saved appearance templates.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game.
[*]Install [url=https://github.com/ngodn/CustomShellSystem/releases]Custom Shell System[/url] and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/mortalshell2/mods/45?tab=files]UE4SS for MS2[/url] if you don't already have them.
[*]Extract [b]CSS_Seductress_V2_eins0fx_P.zip[/b]. Copy the included folder into [b]Sparta/MortalShell2/Content/Paks/~mods/[/b].
[*]Enter the game world, open [b]Inventory → CSS[/b], select [b]Seductress V2[/b] and choose Wear.
[/list]

[code]Sparta/MortalShell2/Content/Paks/~mods/
    CSS_Seductress_V2_eins0fx_P/
        CSS_Seductress_V2_eins0fx_P.pak
        CSS_Seductress_V2_eins0fx_P.utoc
        CSS_Seductress_V2_eins0fx_P.ucas[/code]

Keep all three files together with their original names. The thumbnail, variants and color resources are included. BG3, the original Seductress package and BeauteGenessa are not required downloads.

[size=4][b]Using the wardrobe[/b][/size]

[b]Shell:[/b] Select Seductress V2, then use the variant arrows or D-pad left/right to browse its variants. Use A on controller or the displayed Wear action to equip one.

[b]Color:[/b] Choose a palette or adjust individual parts. A control affects only the parts present in that variant. Original or Reset all colors restores the outfit's original materials.

[b]Templates:[/b] Save a variant and color combination to use again later.

Mouse and keyboard controls are shown in the CSS page. Body shape, skin wetness and physics strength are not adjustable sliders.

[size=4][b]Compatibility and known issues[/b][/size]

Tested with Genessa. Other gameplay shells and every combat or story animation have not been fully tested. Cloth and secondary motion use the existing game and mod animation setup; this package does not include a separate physics engine. Some poses may still clip, and Inventory lighting differs from the game world.

Other CSS outfits can remain installed. Ordinary replacements that overwrite the game's Genessa meshes or materials may affect this outfit. Install only one copy of the V2 package.

To uninstall, select Original appearance or another outfit in CSS, close the game and delete [b]CSS_Seductress_V2_eins0fx_P[/b] from ~mods. Keep CSS if you use other wardrobe outfits.

[size=4][b]Reporting a problem[/b][/size]

Include your CSS and UE4SS versions, selected variant, gameplay shell, other mods and steps to reproduce. Attach a screenshot or short recording, plus CSS.log from ue4ss/Mods/CustomShellSystem/. The [url=https://github.com/ngodn/CustomShellSystem/blob/main/docs/nexus/reporting-problems.bbcode.txt]reporting guide[/url] lists the remaining log locations.
