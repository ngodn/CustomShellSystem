[b]CSSX CINE 1.0.0[/b] (2026-10-03)

Hey everyone. First release of CINE, a cinematic camera for showing off your shells and outfits.

[list]
[*]Seven presets: Fashion Walk 360, Pose Glide 3, 5, 7 and 10 s, Turntable and Photo Orbit.[/*]
[*]Record your own walking route and frame your own last shot.[/*]
[*]A real walking pace on walk takes, not the scripted jog.[/*]
[*]A look track that cycles your CSS palettes during the shot and puts your own look back afterwards.[/*]
[*]Frame guides for 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 2.39:1.[/*]
[*]Presets are plain JSON files you can copy, edit and share.[/*]
[/list]

Requires CSSX 1.3.0. The look track also needs CSS 1.0.0-beta.9.
