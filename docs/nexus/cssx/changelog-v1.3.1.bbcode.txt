[b]CSSX 1.3.1[/b] (2026-10-06)

Number entry for Cheat Menu and other extensions, plus a fix for the controller shortcut that could leave the menu stuck.

[size=4][b]Added[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Extensions can pass an object where a game function expects an interface.[/*]
[*]Extensions can write a large struct property without CSSX reading the whole thing back.[/*]
[*]Extensions can draw guides and prompts while the game's HUD is hidden.[/*]
[*]Extensions can compare average frame time, slow frames and hitches over a chosen measurement window.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Changed[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Type exact numbers in native text fields. Press Enter or click Set value. Escape cancels; controller arrows still work.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Fixed[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Pressing both thumbsticks during play no longer opens the menu and leaves controls stuck. On controller, open the Player Menu and switch to CSSX. F6 still works on keyboard.[/*]
[/list]
