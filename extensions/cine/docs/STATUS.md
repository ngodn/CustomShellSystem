Goal, decisions and checklist: `PHOTO-MODE-GOAL.md`. 2.0.0 now runs inside the game's Photo
Mode (needs CSSX 1.3.2): pause > System > Activate CINE Mode opens Photo Mode with CINE's tabs
(Take, Route, Animation, Scene, Look, Render) built from the game's own widgets. Record Route
and takes step out of Photo Mode (CSSX `photo.step_out` / `photo.step_in`) and come back on
their tab. The Render tab renders a take to `renders/<scene>-<date>/video.mp4` with the bundled
FFmpeg (`tools/fetch_ffmpeg.py`, git-ignored exe), music through `SM_CINE_Render` in the main pak.
Scenes ship as pak packs (`CINE_<Pack>_eins0fx_P`, built by `assets/packs/build_pack.py`,

CINE ships no scenes. `cine-v2.0.0` is a local tag (not pushed) at 3f89e93; the release zip in
`extensions/core/dist/cine-v2.0.0/` is built from it and verified. Ready to ship once the user pushes and uploads.

Verified live 28 Oct: pause row (game confirm input), tabs and rows in the game's look, Record
Route with a scripted walk, final shot from the Photo Mode view, Fashion Walk 360 rolled and
back on the Take tab, close returns the game's Photo Mode untouched. Live 28 Oct (second session): HUD hidden during takes, renders at 30/24 fps, all rows. Live 28 Oct (evening): render music records
(profane-proxima, -14.7 dB peak in music.wav and the video's AAC track) after the pack songs were
re-cooked as streamed audio (569cf09; ForceInline songs never played in the game). Still to check
live: packs loading when CINE Mode is opened right after the save loads (3f89e93), breakdown, label
and guide look after lane E, a pack style override, frame cost in and out of CINE Mode, no-pak fallback.

The three fight presets are held back from 2.0.0 (the code stays). In the live demo on 25 Oct

## Mark of the Beast (26 Oct 2026, unreleased)

A ten-take film for Profane Proxima and Unholy Genessa on Polyphia's Mark of the Beast, cut
9:16 for Instagram first. Goal and song map: `BEAST-GOAL.md`. Presets `beast-p0..p4` and
`beast-g0..g4` are generated by `tools/mark_of_the_beast_film.py`; the audio slices by
`tools/mark_of_the_beast_audio.py`; the edit, narration and cards by
`tools/mark_of_the_beast_post.py`. Takes were recorded with `work/live/record.py`.

The film needed three new look-step fields: `expression` (a facial expression id),
`performance` as a string id with `performance_rate`, and `montage` (one of the game's own
animation montages, `""` stops it) with `montage_rate`. A montage-only sequence runs without
CSS. Tracked shots now anchor on her live position and sample every frame while a performance
plays, with a faster follow when she moves fast, so After Brute Battle stays in frame.
Every wear in a take carries the customization that outfit opened with (hers, or the first-seen
one), so a variant she never customized still gets her settings; see BEAST-GOAL.md.
A montage step or an idle step ends the take's performance for good (no restart between moves),
a step's montage plays after its palette and controls, a montage is stopped before a wear, and
montages are loaded for every play (a cached handle expired between takes).

### Next: Fight Replay (queued)
The user's design: the player fights normally with the game camera while CINE records; then
CINE replays the fight and the cinematic camera films it, any preset, any number of times.
- Unreal's replay system is a dead end here. `demorec` works in shipping (UWorld::Exec, not
  guarded) and recorded a 678 KB replay, but `demoplay` rebuilt an empty world with no pawn:
  MS2 is single player (characters and animation are not replicated) and its World Partition
  world is not rebuilt. `demostop` then loads the main menu. Do not retry it.
- The way forward is CINE's own recorder: sample the player and the target each frame
  (transform and bone pose, natively, not through per-bone bridge calls), then hide them and
  drive posable copies (same mesh and materials) in the live world. Hit effects, blood and
  sound need recording and re-firing separately. Research the game's character and animation
  setup first (CXXHeaderDump, Sparta.hpp).
