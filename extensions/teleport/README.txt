CSSX Traverse
=============

Traverse to any point on the world map. Open the map, hover a beacon, gate,
dungeon or well, and the Traverse prompt appears in the map's prompt bar. Press
T on the keyboard, or click the left stick (L3) on a controller, to travel
there. The jump uses the game's own streaming teleport, so there is no loading
screen, and it works from anywhere on the map, not just points you have already
unlocked.

By default the arrival is a meteor: your character drops from the sky as the
game's own comet, under the game's dive and landing cameras, and touches down
with the 4-point landing, impact effects, rumble and sound. Turn Meteor arrival
off for a plain streaming teleport instead.

Requires CSSX. Install CSSX first, then drop this folder into
Sparta/MortalShell2/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods/CSSX/extensions/ alongside the
other extensions.

Controls
--------
- Hover a point on the world map to show the Traverse prompt.
- Press T (keyboard) or click the left stick / L3 (controller) to traverse.
- With confirmation on, a dialog appears: the dpad or left stick moves between
  Traverse and Cancel, the confirm button traverses, the back button cancels.

Settings (CSSX tab > Traverse)
------------------------------
- Map traverse: turn the whole feature on or off.
- Confirmation: ask before traversing, or go on the first press.
- Allow locked points: traverse to points you have not unlocked yet. Turn this
  off to show the prompt only where the game already lets you fast travel.
- Meteor arrival: arrive as a falling meteor, or with a plain streaming
  teleport. Works the same on a stock shell or a CSS custom shell.
