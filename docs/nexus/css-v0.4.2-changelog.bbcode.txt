[b]CSS 0.4.2[/b]

A major performance and quality-of-life update for Custom Shell System. Features critical frame-budget optimizations, automatic outfit carry-over when severing into Harbinger form, teleporter appearance fixes, and extension library presentation improvements.

[size=4][b]Performance & Unreal Engine Optimizations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]In-Memory Unreal Asset Caching:[/b] Added an in-memory asset cache ([code]s_asset_cache[/code]) and cached Kismet library reference for all Unreal asset lookups. Eliminates redundant [code]MakeSoftObjectPath[/code], [code]Conv_SoftObjPathToSoftObjRef[/code], [code]LoadAsset_Blocking[/code], and [code]StaticFindObject[/code] engine calls during outfit application and texture operations.
[*][b]Cosmetic Maintenance Rate-Limiting (~7 Hz):[/b] Material-reset checks and menu-preview synchronization previously ran on every single frame tick at 60/120/144 FPS. They are now rate-limited to ~7 Hz (150 ms intervals). Gameplay material resets are still corrected imperceptibly within 150 ms while taking cosmetic checks completely off the per-frame rendering budget.
[*][b]Exception-Free Material Hierarchy Resolution:[/b] Replaced recursive, exception-throwing dynamic material unwrapping in [code]repair_materials_needed[/code] with a fast, non-throwing iterative parent traversal ([code]material_base_asset[/code]), eliminating costly C++ exception-handling unwinds during cosmetic reconciliation.
[*][b]Status Publication Throttling:[/b] Status state publication is now event-driven and throttled to 500 ms intervals instead of evaluating and publishing state every frame tick, eliminating continuous filesystem overhead and disk thrashing.
[*][b]Zero-Allocation UI Texture Dimensions:[/b] Image and banner dimensions are probed once and cached in memory, ensuring zero heap allocations and zero disk I/O on per-frame UI ticks.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Harbinger Outfit Carry-Over (Mid-Combat Severing)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Automatic Outfit Transfer on Death:[/b] When you die and sever into the bare Harbinger form ([code]Darkform[/code]), CSS now automatically carries over the custom outfit worn by your living shell. In the heat of combat you no longer have to pause, open the menu, and re-apply your look manually.
[*][b]Toggleable in Wardrobe:[/b] Added a new setting at the top of Tab 1: [b]Harbinger outfit[/b] ([i]Carry from shell / Keeps its own[/i]). If you prefer the Harbinger to retain its own distinct saved look, you can toggle it back with a single click.
[/list]

[size=4][b]World Transitions & Teleporter Fixes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Appearance Preserved Across Portals and Jump Points:[/b] Fast-travel teleporters, portals, and jump points in Mortal Shell II re-attach meshes and reset skeletal mesh properties, which previously could cause outfits to drop back to the base shell or corrupt textures. The mesh reconciliation pipeline now detects and prevents resets across world transitions and respawns.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Wardrobe Usability[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Equipped Outfit Pinned to Top (Tab-Exit Stabilization):[/b] The outfit currently worn by your shell is automatically pinned near the top of the list (right after the Harbinger toggle and Original Appearance). The list ordering is now stabilized while actively browsing the SHELL tab—selecting an outfit or cycling through variants will never jump items out from under your cursor or disrupt selection. The newly equipped outfit pins smoothly to the top once you leave the view.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Extension Library Presentation & Banner Scaling[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Card Banners Retain True Proportions:[/b] Fixed a presentation bug where extension card banners were horizontally stretched on wide and ultrawide viewports. 3:1 banners, crests, and circular emblems now maintain their intended geometric proportions across all display ratios (16:9, 16:10, 21:9 ultrawide, and 32:9).
[*][b]Square Icon Protection:[/b] If an extension manifest references a 1:1 square icon instead of a panoramic banner, CSSX now centers the icon squarely rather than stretching it across the 3x3 card slot.
[*][b]Clean Header Logo Compositing:[/b] Eliminated unwanted background fill artifacts behind the CSSX header logo, preserving clean transparent alpha blending over the dark inventory canvas.
[*][b]CSSX ABI 2 Retained HUD Integration:[/b] Full engine coordination with CSSX 0.3.1 for high-performance HUD overlays (including the new Compass & Minimap extension).
[/list]

[b]Updating from 0.4.0 or 0.4.1:[/b]
Close the game and extract the ZIP into [code]MortalShell2/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods/[/code]
Replace the CSS runtime files. Keep your [b]CustomShellSystem/state/[/b] folder, existing outfit packages, CSSX, and installed extensions.
