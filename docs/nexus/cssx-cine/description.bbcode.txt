Hey everyone. CINE is a CSSX extension for making showcase videos of your character. You pick a camera preset, CINE moves the camera (and walks your character if the preset needs it), and you record it with whatever recorder you already use: OBS, ShadowPlay, Steam, anything. CINE doesn't record video itself.

If you also run CSS (the wardrobe), CINE can flip through your outfit's palettes during the shot and put your own look back afterwards. Without CSS the camera still works fine, you just don't get the palette part.

[size=4][b]What's in it[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Fashion Walk 360[/b]: she walks a route you recorded while the camera circles her once (front, right, behind, left, front again), then it settles on the last shot you framed yourself.
[*][b]Pose Glide 3 / 5 / 7 / 10 s[/b]: for idle poses. One smooth arc from high behind her left shoulder round to her front-left. It's measured from her head, so it works for standing, sitting and kneeling poses. The longer ones start wider and higher.
[*][b]Turntable[/b]: a slow 12 second circle at full-body distance, good for outfit showcases.
[*][b]Photo Orbit[/b]: holds the camera on 7 angles, 2.5 seconds each, so you can grab stills with your own screenshot key.
[/list]

Other stuff:
[list]
[*]Walk takes use the game's real walk, not the scripted jog. She starts slow, speeds up to walking pace, and slows down near the end.
[*]The game HUD is hidden while you're in Cine World, and CINE draws nothing on screen during a take.
[*]Frame guides (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 2.39:1) to help you frame before you roll. They go away when the take starts.
[*]Before a take, CINE checks the camera path for walls and pulls the camera in where it would clip through one (you can turn that off).
[*]Distance, Height and Field of view sliders apply on top of any preset.
[*]A countdown (0 to 10 s, shown small in a corner) before each take so you have time to hit record. Trim it out afterwards.
[*]Presets are plain .cine.json files in the presets folder. Copy one, tweak it, hit Reload presets. A broken file gets skipped and named in the CSSX log.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How to use it[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Open CSSX (F6, or the CSSX tab in the Player Menu) and go to CINE.
[*]For a walk shot: Route > Record route. Walk the path you want (at least 3 m), then stand still for 3 seconds. It saves on its own.
[*]Put her where the walk should end, frame her with the normal game camera, and use Route > Use the current view as final shot (or F7 in Cine World).
[*]Pick a preset, hit Enter Cine World, start your recorder, press F8.
[/list]

Keys in Cine World: [b]F8[/b] rolls a take, [b]F9[/b] cancels a take or leaves Cine World, [b]F10[/b] cycles the frame guide, [b]F7[/b] saves the final shot. Keyboard only, because the D-pad uses items in this game.

For the palette part, set Look track to Cycle palettes (it starts on Off). After the take your own look comes back and gets saved like normal.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]You need CSSX 1.3.0 or newer.
[*]Optional: CSS 1.0.0-beta.9 or newer for the palette part.
[*]Close the game and extract the [b]eins0fx.cine[/b] folder into [b]ue4ss/Mods/CSSX/extensions/[/b].
[/list]

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Opening a menu, pressing F9, a loading screen, travel or death all cancel a take. The camera and walk settings go back, and so does your look (after a loading screen CINE keeps trying until CSS is ready again).
[*]On walk takes she just follows the path you recorded. Clear the enemies first and record over open ground.
[/list]
