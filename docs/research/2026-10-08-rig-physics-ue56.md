# Rig and physics repair research, UE 5.6

Research date: 2026-10-08. Scope: evidence for a proposed repair goal covering CSS Eve's six outfits, Unholy Genessa and Commander White. This is research, not implementation or visual acceptance. No game interaction, runtime edit, asset edit, install or goal activation was performed.

## Evidence boundary

Epic links below request `application_version=5.6`; they explain the documented feature families. A version selector does not prove every online example matches the installed patch. Exact implementation statements below come from the local engine whose [Build.version][build] declares **5.6.1, CL44394996, branch ++UE5+Release-5.6**. That authoring build identity does not establish binary identity with the shipped game's engine.

The local KawaiiPhysics research checkout declares **1.21.0**, has HEAD `0035f52`, and was clean when inspected. It is separate evidence from Epic engine source and is not proof that the game ships that plugin revision. See its [descriptor][kawaii-version]. Existing CSS findings are historical project evidence, not a fresh reproduction: [skeleton foundation](../development/skeleton-foundation-research.md), [aligned-hand calibration](../development/aligned-hand-calibration.md), and [outfit index](../development/eve-outfit-index.md).

## What the repair must distinguish

| Layer | Evidence and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Skin weights and mesh bind reference | Weights determine which bones deform each vertex; UE normalizes their total. Inspect joint placement, local axes, normalized influences, twist distribution and seams before attributing a collapsed wrist or elbow to physics. [Epic: Skeleton Editing](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/skeleton-editing-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.6). Exact 5.6.1 [SkeletalRender.cpp][skin], `UpdateRefToLocalMatricesInner`, lines 357-462, combines current component transforms with inverse bind matrices. Changing bind transforms while retaining old geometry/weights is therefore a deformation change, not metadata cleanup. |
| Animation Skeleton and retargeting | Traditional translation modes and compatible-Skeleton rotation remapping are different operations. [Epic: Animation Retargeting](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/animation-retargeting-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.6). Exact 5.6.1 [SkeletonRemapping.cpp][remap], lines 95-174, derives rotation-frame corrections from source/target reference poses. [AnimationRuntime.cpp][retarget], lines 2900-2951, applies compatible remapping before selecting translation modes, including the source-mode flag. Matching names or enabling compatibility does not certify correct deformation. Preserve and record both Skeleton reference and mesh bind reference, authored animation source, effective modes, parent mapping and additive conventions. |
| Virtual bones and hand/foot IK | Virtual bones provide moving reference frames and require graph use to affect the result; they are not a universal contact repair. Epic's example protects virtual targets from an additive layer, then applies IK. [Epic: Virtual Bones](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/virtual-bones-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.6). Exact 5.6.1 [Two Bone IK][ik], lines 169-190, outputs upper limb, lower limb and end-bone transforms. Small hand-target error cannot establish finger closure, wrist contour or weapon clearance. Retain game graph order and intentional release phases; assess feet against contact phase, floor and footwear geometry. |
| Sockets and proportions | Socket offsets are relative to their parent bone; mesh-specific sockets can distinguish meshes sharing a Skeleton. Bone names alone do not preserve a socket's transform. [Epic: Skeletal Mesh Sockets](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/skeletal-mesh-sockets-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.6). Inspect resolved socket ownership, parent, local transform and world attachment against each grip. An IK Retargeter is an available alternative for differing hierarchies/proportions, not evidence that the game uses one. [Epic: IK Rig Retargeting](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/ik-rig-animation-retargeting-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.6). |

Recommended diagnostic order: record current assets and graph ownership; reproduce the unmodified animation pose; inspect deformation and attachment fit; then isolate secondary motion. This is an inference from the separate engine paths above. Do not replace the foundation, add IK everywhere, or add a solver before a controlled comparison identifies the failing layer.

## Collision and motion are separate responsibilities

| System | What the evidence supports | What still needs asset/runtime proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physics Asset | Defines rigid bodies and constraints for skeletal collision and physical simulation. [Epic: Physics Asset Editor](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/physics-asset-editor-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.6). | Assignment alone does not establish active simulation, suitable body fit or that another solver consumes these bodies. Audit shapes, bone attachment, enabled pairs, constraints and actual consumer. A body collider does not repair a bad skin weight. |
| Chaos Cloth | The exact 5.6.1 [collider implementation][collider], `ExtractPhysicsAssetCollision` from line 520, extracts physics-asset collision; separate paths build ordinary and skinned collision geometry. Cloth runs through its own [simulation implementation][cloth-sim]. | Identify the garment's actual clothing asset/component path, physical mesh, pin/constraint maps, collider source, render mapping and LODs. Existing collision types do not guarantee protection from penetration in fast motion, good silhouette, or acceptable cost. Keep prior rejected collision trials in the record. |
| AnimDynamics | AnimGraph secondary bone motion with constraints and explicit planar/spherical limits. Chains require more iterations than isolated bodies; overlapping constraints can cause instability. [Epic: AnimDynamics](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/animation-blueprint-animdynamics-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.6). | Its limits should not be mistaken for automatic full Physics Asset or scene collision. Verify bound chain, box/joint settings, simulation space, limits and blend/LOD behavior. It can receive gravity/external force, so a static upstream pose does not prove the solver is disabled. |
| KawaiiPhysics or existing body springs | KawaiiPhysics is a bone-physics plugin. Its [author's parameters](https://github.com/pafuhana1213/KawaiiPhysics/wiki/Parameters) include collision, movement, reset and runtime-update controls. Local 1.21.0 [collision source][kawaii-collision], lines 71-140, converts configured Physics Asset sphere/capsule/box geometry into node limits; world sweeps are a separate path from line 317. | Confirm the shipped plugin/node version and existing graph before adopting any current API or tuning. Do not copy numerical stiffness/damping between different solvers as if units and integration match. Map hair roots/tips, accessories and body spring regions to their existing owner; avoid competing solvers writing the same bones. |

The proposed goal should require an explicit solver/region map for each character and outfit. Hair, a fitted bodice, loose sleeves, a skirt, wings and body springs need different checks. Existing acceptable motion should be retained unless the matched comparison establishes a defect. No blanket switch to Chaos Cloth, AnimDynamics or KawaiiPhysics follows from this research.

## Morphs, cloth and fitted collision

Exact 5.6.1 contains cloth simulation morph support, but **that is not automatic compatibility with every skeletal-mesh morph**. [ChaosClothingSimulationMesh.cpp][cloth-mesh], lines 190-240, applies one selected morph index/weight when its position, normal and index arrays are present and consistent. The [base mesh interface][cloth-interface], lines 145-174, otherwise returns empty morph data; the legacy [FClothingSimulationSkeletalMesh adapter][legacy-cloth] does not override those morph accessors. The separate ChaosClothAsset module contains a [simulation-morph collection facade][sim-morph]. These paths must not be conflated.

For the goal, inventory which path each garment actually uses. Preserve body morph names, ranges, vertex correspondence and garment render/simulation mapping. Check default, individual limits and permitted combinations with cloth both disabled and enabled. Inspect collider clearance after the body shape changes; a morph that expands visible skin does not itself demonstrate matching collider expansion. If reposing or rebuilding geometry is necessary, require coherent body, garment and morph-delta transfer plus fresh import/cook readback. This is a proposed validation requirement, not a claim that all combinations currently work or fail.

## World updates, resets and performance

Epic explains that tick groups and prerequisites control same-frame data dependencies. [Epic: Actor Ticking](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/actor-ticking-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.6). Exact 5.6.1 [SkeletalMeshComponent.cpp][component], lines 445-449 and 765-773, schedules cloth from PrePhysics through PostPhysics and adds the component and EndPhysics prerequisites. Lines 3690-3713 route animation dynamics resets to main, linked and post-process instances. In [SkeletalMeshComponentPhysics.cpp][component-physics], lines 3556-3580, visibility settings gate cloth ticking, with teleport-and-reset requested when skipped. Lines 3763-3775 expose distinct cloth teleport and teleport-and-reset requests.

The [Chaos simulation][cloth-sim], lines 524-551, separately handles teleport and reset. AnimDynamics has separate reset/teleport initialization branches in [AnimNode_AnimDynamics.cpp][animdynamics-source], lines 689-859. Local Kawaii 1.21.0 [node code][kawaii-node], lines 229-236, 350-357 and 560-590, also distinguishes reset from teleport handling. Therefore, a proposed swap/respawn/preview transition fix must name the affected component and solver and prove its lifecycle behavior. Calling an API named reset is not evidence that all solver state was reset.

Proposed later acceptance should compare the same asset/pose/morph state in preview and gameplay, and include movement, abrupt turns, attacks, stops, teleport/respawn, outfit changes, preview reopen, camera visibility changes and LOD transitions. Record graph/post-process assignment, component transform/scale, tick prerequisites, active solver, reset reason and asset identity. Use timestamped continuous footage and native telemetry together, because sampled poses can miss transient failures. No such runtime test was performed for this research note.

Performance acceptance should use a matched baseline and candidate with the same scene, camera, frame cap and active characters. Record animation/cloth CPU cost, frame-time distribution and spikes around transitions, simulation vertices, chain lengths, collider counts, iterations/substeps and LOD policy. Exact cloth skinning has trace/cycle-stat instrumentation at [lines 190-193][cloth-mesh]; local Kawaii exposes solver/collision counters in its [node implementation][kawaii-node]. More collision detail, chains or substeps need measured benefit. Do not promise a percentage improvement or a zero-penetration guarantee before measurement.

## Requirements to carry into the proposed goal

1. Keep all six Eve outfits, Unholy Genessa and Commander White separately identifiable. A shared-body pass does not accept every garment or accessory.
2. Link each visible defect to reviewed video, timestamp and state; label an unobserved cause as a hypothesis. Separate wrong pose, poor skinning, attachment error, garment fit, collision failure and solver instability.
3. Preserve accepted materials, morphs, sockets, virtual bones, animation metadata and working secondary motion while comparing one repair layer at a time.
4. Require before/after continuous motion plus geometry/contact evidence, import/cook identity and performance evidence. State unsupported cases and residual defects explicitly.
5. Treat the result as a draft goal until the user authorizes implementation. This note supplies mechanisms and gates, not a diagnosis of footage or approval to modify assets.
