# Compass & Minimap Banner

`banner-v1.png` is an original generated library-card graphic, created on 18 September 2026. The image is 2172 by 724 pixels (3:1 aspect ratio) and has no baked-in title or version; CSSX renders those dynamically.

## Prompt and Design Concept
Inspired by the restrained antique brass and charcoal interface of Mortal Shell II:
- Wide panoramic horizontal composition matching the 3:1 card banner format.
- Centered ornate antique bronze and tarnished gold celestial compass rose with refined dial rings and cardinal headings (N, S, W, E).
- Horizontal wings featuring etched topographic elevation contours, celestial star charts, and subtle golden coordinate markings.
- Deep near-black weathered obsidian stone texture with warm amber rim lighting and quiet bronze haze.
- Dark vignette edges fading into near-black charcoal slate at the perimeter.
- No characters, no game screenshots, no modern HUD elements, and no text (title and version are rendered separately by CSSX).
