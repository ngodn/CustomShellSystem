[b]CSSX Traverse 1.0.1[/b]

[size=4][b]Fixes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]A traverse now tells the game which zone you are arriving in, the way the game's own beacons and gates do. Before, the world stayed in the zone you left: enemies spawned wrong, and a beacon trip taken from that state could corrupt the save so it crashed on every load.[/*]
[*]Traverse waits until a previous traverse has fully arrived before it fires again.[/*]
[*]Traverse refuses to fire from inside a dungeon. Leave through the dungeon's own exit first.[/*]
[*]A point whose zone cannot be read is skipped, with a note in the CSSX tab, instead of fired blind.[/*]
[/list]

Requires CSSX 1.2.1 or newer. Install: extract into [font=Courier New]Sparta/MortalShell2/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods/CSSX/extensions/[/font], overwriting the previous version.
