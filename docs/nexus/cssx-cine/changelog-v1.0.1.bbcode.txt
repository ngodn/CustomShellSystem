[b]CSSX CINE 1.0.1[/b] (2026-10-03)

Hey everyone. First release of CINE on Nexus. It's a CSSX extension for making showcase videos of your character. You record it with your own recorder, CINE handles the camera.

[list]
[*]7 presets: Fashion Walk 360, Pose Glide 3 / 5 / 7 / 10 s, Turntable and Photo Orbit.[/*]
[*]Record your own walking route and frame your own last shot.[/*]
[*]Walk takes use the game's real walk, not the scripted jog.[/*]
[*]With CSS installed, Look track > Cycle palettes flips through your palettes during the shot and puts your own look back after. It starts on Off, so nothing touches your look unless you turn it on.[/*]
[*]Your look goes back even when a take gets cut off by a loading screen, travel or death.[/*]
[*]Frame guides for 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 2.39:1.[/*]
[*]Presets are plain JSON files, so you can tweak and share them.[/*]
[/list]

Needs CSSX 1.3.0. The palette part also needs CSS 1.0.0-beta.9.
