[b]CSSX 1.3.0[/b] (2026-10-03)

Hey everyone. This update brings a new extension, CINE, plus a way for mods to talk to each other.

[size=4][b]What's new[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]CINE, a cinematic camera for showcase videos.[/b] Record the walk you want, frame the last shot, pick a preset and press F8. The camera flies the shot and she walks at a real walking pace. Record it with your own recorder. It's a separate download with its own page.[/*]
[*][b]Mods can offer services to CSSX extensions.[/b] A native mod exports one function, and extensions find and call it. Neither side needs the other installed. CINE uses this to change CSS looks during a take. CSSX itself doesn't know about any particular mod.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Nerd corner[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Two new bridge ops: [b]service.list[/b] and [b]service.call {service, version, request}[/b]. Providers export [b]cssx_services()[/b], and the contract is a small C header (include/cssx/service.h) that providers copy. CSSX asks the provider for its table before every call, so a mod that hot-swaps its DLL is never called stale. Details are in docs/abi.md.[/*]
[/list]
